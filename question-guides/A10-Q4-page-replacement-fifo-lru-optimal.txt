xv6 OS LAB - ASSIGNMENT 10, A10 Q4: Page replacement: FIFO, LRU, Optimal
================================================================

WHAT THE QUESTION ASKS
----------------------
All three algorithms in one user-space simulator on the same standard string: per-reference frames and hit/fault, totals for 3 frames, then a 3/4/5-frame comparison table with hits, hit ratio and fault ratio, and which algorithm wins.

HOW TO BUILD AND RUN
--------------------
Build and boot:   make qemu CPUS=1
At the xv6 shell: pagereplace

WHICH FILES TO OPEN / SHOW
--------------------------
pagereplace.c (user space only)

EXPECTED OUTPUT
---------------
Optimal fewest faults, LRU next, FIFO most on the standard string; the table prints all three at 3, 4 and 5 frames.

IN THE LAB: WHAT TO DO AND WHAT TO SHOW
---------------------------------------
Pair with A9 Q3: this adds Optimal and the hit/fault ratios. Screenshot the table for your report.

HOW IT IS IMPLEMENTED
---------------------
Extend the A9 Q3 simulator with Optimal: evict the page whose next use is furthest in the future (look ahead in the reference string). Then loop all three algorithms over 3, 4 and 5 frames and tabulate faults, hits and ratios.
