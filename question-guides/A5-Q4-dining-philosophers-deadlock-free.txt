xv6 OS LAB - ASSIGNMENT 5, A5 Q4: Dining philosophers (deadlock-free)
================================================================

WHAT THE QUESTION ASKS
----------------------
Five philosophers, five forks as binary semaphores, cycles of thinking, hungry, eating. Must use a deadlock-avoidance strategy; this tree uses resource ordering (always pick the lower-numbered fork first).

HOW TO BUILD AND RUN
--------------------
Build and boot:   make qemu CPUS=1
At the xv6 shell: dining

WHICH FILES TO OPEN / SHOW
--------------------------
support/sem.c, dining.c (fork ordering per philosopher)

EXPECTED OUTPUT
---------------
All five philosophers complete their cycles with no hang and no starvation over the run.

IN THE LAB: WHAT TO DO AND WHAT TO SHOW
---------------------------------------
Be ready to explain why picking the lower-numbered fork first breaks the circular wait. Run dining, screenshot the transitions.

HOW IT IS IMPLEMENTED
---------------------
One binary semaphore per fork, all starting at 1. Each philosopher picks up the lower-numbered of its two forks first, then the higher one, eats, and puts both down. That global ordering is what removes the circular wait.
