xv6 OS LAB - ASSIGNMENT 10, A10 Q3: pageinfo and setreadonly
================================================================

WHAT THE QUESTION ASKS
----------------------
pageinfo() shows whether an address is mapped plus its frame and P/W/U flags; setreadonly() clears PTE_W on the page. Writing afterwards must fault.

HOW TO BUILD AND RUN
--------------------
Build and boot:   make qemu CPUS=1
At the xv6 shell: pagetest

WHICH FILES TO OPEN / SHOW
--------------------------
vm.c (pageinfo, setreadonly), sysproc.c

EXPECTED OUTPUT
---------------
The page shows writable, then read-only after setreadonly, and the attempted write faults and the process is killed.

IN THE LAB: WHAT TO DO AND WHAT TO SHOW
---------------------------------------
Find where the fault lands in trap.c and why the process is killed rather than the page silently re-writable. Run pagetest, screenshot.

HOW IT IS IMPLEMENTED
---------------------
pageinfo(): walk to the PTE and print the frame and P/W/U flags. setreadonly(): walk to the same entry and clear PTE_W. The next write faults because the hardware checks that bit on every store; the fault handler kills the process.
